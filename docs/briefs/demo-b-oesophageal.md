## Facts to use (all UNVERIFIED — partner to confirm)
- **F1** Drinking very hot beverages (above 65 °C) is classified by the WHO's International Agency for Research on Cancer as "probably carcinogenic to humans" (Group 2A), based on associations with oesophageal cancer; the classification says nothing about the size of the risk. Source: IARC press release pr244 (June 2016), https://www.iarc.who.int/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/pr244_E.pdf
- **F2** *(only needed for option D)* Mainland critical-illness cover uses standardised definitions (2020 revision): 28 severe + 3 mild conditions; cancer is graded severe or mild. Source: 中国保险行业协会《重大疾病保险的疾病定义使用规范（2020年修订版）》, https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/11/5/art_8616_104704.html

## Must avoid
- Company, product, plan or scheme brand names — generic terms only
- Fear tactics or implying the tea habit "will" cause cancer (F1 is a probability classification, not a prediction)
- Jokes about symptoms, treatment, patients or death
- Medical advice beyond "see a doctor / get checked"
- Promising that any claim will be paid; any statistic without a source
