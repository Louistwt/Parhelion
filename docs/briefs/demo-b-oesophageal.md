## Notes for you (not part of the brief)

### Why these choices
- **News presenter** keeps one anchor in a plain studio: few locations, consistent shots, and the key point can be a
  clean text comparison next to the anchor (added in editing, never generated).
- **Existing characters**: 小朱 (`smallpig`, in his new look `anchor`: suit and tie, same face) is the anchor, 小晶 (`siuming`) the hotpot-and-drinks man. Both have
  approved realistic sheets and voices from Demo A.
- **30 s → stage 0 only.** Research showed that for oesophageal cancer both sides pay 100% from invasive stage I; the
  difference is at **stage 0 (in situ)**, which the mainland 2020 standard doesn't cover (F2, F3) while many HK plans
  pay part (F4, F5). Repeat cancer claims in some HK plans are another difference, dropped to keep one point.
- **Alcohol** is the habit with a firm source (IARC Group 1, F1). **Spicy food is not an established cause** (China
  Kadoorie Biobank: inverse association, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7938514/; a case-control
  meta-analysis cited in the NCC 2022 guideline: OR 1.52), so it stays scenery with no claim.
- **HK changed on 1 Sep 2026:** the Hong Kong Federation of Insurers' voluntary CI definitions apply to new products
  only; existing policies keep their own definitions.

### Other key points on file (not chosen)
- Repeat cancer claims (some HK plans pay again after a first cancer; product-specific).
- Guaranteed renewal into old age (HK medical plans) vs mainland one-year medical plans.
- Access to newer cancer drugs in HK vs the mainland reimbursement catalogue.

### HK brochures checked (for your checking only; never name them in a video)
- https://www.cigna.com.hk/en/medical-insurance/vitalguard-critical-illness (20% per early-stage claim; CIS twice in different organs)
- https://www.wli.com.hk/en/productWellProtectCriticalIIP.jhtml (20% early stage; up to 4 further cancer claims at 100%, capped at 400%)

## Facts to use (all UNVERIFIED — sourced by Claude 2026-09-29; partner may correct later)
- **F1** Alcohol consumption is classified by IARC as Group 1 (carcinogenic to humans); the oesophagus is among the sites. Source: IARC press release 196 (2 Nov 2009), https://www.iarc.who.int/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/pr196_E.pdf
- **F2** Mainland: the 2020 industry standard (中保协《重大疾病保险的疾病定义使用规范（2020年修订版）》) has 28 severe + 3 mild conditions. 恶性肿瘤——重度 covers invasive malignant tumours (ICD-O-3 behaviour code 3, 6, 9) and excludes 原位癌, 癌前病变, 非浸润性癌 and 上皮内瘤变. In AJCC 8th ed., oesophageal Tis (high-grade dysplasia) is stage 0. Sources: https://www.iachina.cn/module/download/downfile.jsp?filename=dfbe46c8f7aa414694e7043603c6fb0b.pdf (§3.1.1.1), https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/11/5/art_8616_104705.html , https://www.cancer.gov/types/esophageal/hp/esophageal-treatment-pdq
- **F3** Mainland: 恶性肿瘤——轻度 is a closed list of six cancers (e.g. stage I thyroid, T1N0M0 prostate); oesophagus is not on it and 原位癌 is excluded. A mild condition may pay at most 30% of the severe sum insured (a cap, not a typical rate). Insurers may add in-situ cover themselves. Source: same standard, §2.2 and §3.1.2.1.
- **F4** HK: the Hong Kong Federation of Insurers' voluntary Best Practice on standardised CI definitions (16 major + 5 minor conditions) applies to new products launched on or after 1 Sep 2026 and does not change existing policies. Major Cancer excludes carcinoma in situ; carcinoma-in-situ is a separate minor condition. Sources: https://ci-definitions.hkfi.org.hk/best-practice , https://mini-sites.oss-cn-hongkong.aliyuncs.com/critical-illness/Best%20Practice%20on%20Standardization%20of%20CI%20definitions_bilingual.pdf
- **F5** HK market practice: plans reviewed by the Consumer Council paid 10–30% of the sum insured for carcinoma in situ / early-stage cancer, limited to 2–3 claims in different organs. Source (2017, dated): https://www.consumer.org.hk/en/press-release/483-cancer-insurance
- **F7** Background, not for the script unless needed: most oesophageal cancer patients in China are diagnosed at a middle or late stage (National Cancer Center 2022 screening guideline, 中国肿瘤 2022;31(6)); China had about 43.8% of the world's new cases in 2022 (GLOBOCAN 2022, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39668405/).

**Not confirmed:** whether 10–30% is still typical in HK in 2026; whether oesophageal in-situ is on every HK plan's organ list; how many mainland products add in-situ cover themselves; any Insurance Authority guidance on payouts.

## Must avoid
- Company, product, plan or brand names; any real news channel's name, logo, ticker or "breaking news" styling
- Saying or implying spicy food causes cancer (evidence is conflicting; the China Kadoorie Biobank found an inverse association). Spicy hotpot is lifestyle scenery only [TODO: humans — keep or drop]
- Saying the habits *will* cause cancer; fear or pressure tactics
- Implying anyone delays treatment for a payout (only the payout rule waits; stage 0 is treated when found)
- Jokes about symptoms, treatment, patients or death; hospital or illness imagery
- Medical advice beyond "get checked"; promising any claim will be paid
- Text inside images (the panel is added in editing)
